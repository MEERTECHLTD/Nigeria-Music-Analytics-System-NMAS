### What this measures, and what it does not

- Streaming revenue is estimated from observed audience levels and observed volumes using published per-unit rates. No platform reports its payouts to this system.
- Employment is a **national** figure for the whole sector. It is not these artists' employees.
- The domestic/export split is measured in 23 of 31 quarters; the rest are blank.
