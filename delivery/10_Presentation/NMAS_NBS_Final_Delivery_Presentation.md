## Slide E8 — Methodology in One Page
**Content (verbatim from `NBS_METHODOLOGY_AND_SOURCES.md`):**
1. **Streaming Revenue (USD):** `Spotify monthly listeners × 3.5 × 3 months × $0.004 + YouTube actual daily views × $0.004 + Deezer fans × 2 × 3 months × $0.004 + other platforms (30% of Spotify component)`.
2. **Export Revenue:** Total streaming × 70%, where 70% is the non-Nigerian share derived from Chartmetric *Where People Listen* (30% domestic, per WIPO 2025 international music trade methodology).
3. **Employment:** Baseline 300,000 direct + 1,000,000 indirect (US ITA Nigeria 2024); 62% / 38% male/female split (UNESCO 2023); 2% quarter-over-quarter growth (Nairametrics, Vanguard).
4. **Costs:** 131 artists × 2 tracks per quarter × {Production ₦750,000 + Distribution ₦15,000 + Hosting ₦50,000/qtr + Promotion ₦250,000} = ₦272,480,000 per quarter.
5. **Currency:** Fixed exchange rate ₦1,500/USD throughout the delivery (see Slide E9).
**Speaker notes:** Read the formulas slowly. State plainly: streaming revenue is a *modelled* indicator (listener-to-stream proxy × payout coefficient), not a settlement figure. Export share is derived from listener geography. Employment and costs are sector benchmarks, not panel-derived.

## Slide E9 — Conversion and Standardisation Logic
**Content:**
- **Per-stream rate (Spotify, YouTube, Deezer):** $0.004 (Ditto Music 2026; cross-checked against Royalty Exchange 2025, LabelGrid 2026, Hootsuite 2025, RouteNote 2025, Soundcamps 2026).
- **Listener→stream proxy:** Spotify monthly listeners × 3.5 listens/month (assumption, not observed); Deezer fans × 2 listens/month; YouTube uses *actual* daily views, no proxy.
- **"Other platforms" allowance:** flat 30% of the Spotify revenue component to represent Apple Music, Pandora, TikTok, etc., which Chartmetric does not expose at track level (see denied-endpoints list).
- **Domestic / Export split:** 30% / 70% derived from Chartmetric `where-people-listen` aggregated across the panel.
- **Currency:** All NGN figures use a constant ₦1,500/USD throughout (no time-varying FX). This is deliberate: it isolates real activity changes from FX noise. Volatility risk is flagged in Slide E11.
- **Geo normalisation:** ISO country codes from Chartmetric payloads; Nigerian-city listening only retained from Where People Listen for the Nigeria-specific share.
- **Date harmonisation:** All daily observations stamped to UTC date; quarterly rules applied per variable (`sum`, `net_change`, `last_value`) — see Technical Slide T3.
**Speaker notes:** Be candid: 3.5 and 2 listens/month and the 30% "other platforms" are coefficients, not measurements. They were chosen to align with Ditto/Royalty Exchange/IFPI references but should be revisited periodically.
**Visual:** Conversion-pipeline diagram: raw API counters → coefficient layer → currency layer → indicator.
**Evidence:** `NBS_METHODOLOGY_AND_SOURCES.md`; `Variable_Methodology.md` (Where_People_Listen, Spotify_monthly_listeners_daily, YouTube_artist_daily_views, Deezer_fans_daily); `nbs_deliverables.py` / `nbs_final_delivery.py`.

## Slide E13 — Recommendations to NBS and Conclusion
**Content:**
1. **Adopt the four indicators as experimental statistics** under an "experimental / modelled" classification, with the formulas on Slide E8 published verbatim alongside.
2. **Replace the fixed FX coefficient with the CBN official rate series**, applied per quarter; preserve the constant-FX series in parallel for real-activity readings.
3. **Replace the listener→stream proxy (3.5×, 2×) with vendor-confirmed payout statements** for at least the top decile of artists; use the proxy only for the long tail.
4. **Negotiate Chartmetric access tier upgrade** (or expand SoundCharts coverage) to retire denied track-level endpoints — this removes the 30% "other platforms" coefficient.
5. **Expand the universe** from 131 curated artists toward a full Nigerian Spotify/YouTube panel; document the inclusion rule.
6. **Move employment from baseline-projection to a survey-anchored series** by joining NLFS module data to the music-sector classification.
7. **Operate the system on a quarterly cadence** with the existing job-runner (`extraction_jobs`, `job_runs`, `job_checkpoints` tables), with each release accompanied by an extraction log and quality-check report (already templated).
**Conclusion:** The delivery provides a documented, reproducible, source-traceable measurement layer for Nigeria's digital music economy. It is ready for adoption as an experimental statistical product; the recommendations above identify the path to full official-statistic status.

## Slide E14 — AI/ML Disclosure and Tooling Transparency
**Content:** A deliberate, up-front statement of where AI/ML does and does not sit in this system. This slide exists because statistical defensibility requires naming model-based inputs explicitly.

- **Aggregation pipeline (NMAS): deterministic, not ML-based.** Extraction, normalisation, coverage-gap detection, quarterly aggregation, and indicator formulas are all rule-based SQL and arithmetic. There are no classifiers, no embeddings, no generative models, and no fuzzy matching in the NMAS measurement path. Every published figure is reproducible byte-for-byte from stored raw API payloads using the formulas on Slide E8.
- **Entity resolution is exact, not learned.** Artist matching uses Chartmetric's integer artist ID as a hard unique key (`UniqueConstraint("chartmetric_artist_id")`); human-approved manual corrections are captured in the `entity_resolution_overrides` table with an approver and reason.
- **Upstream ML we *consume as inputs*, and how we handle it:**
  - **Spotify `popularity` index** (0–100) — an opaque Spotify-computed score, documented as such in `Variable_Methodology.md` (`Status: observed; Coverage limitations: Popularity is an opaque Spotify-computed index`). We store it but do **not** use it to compute monetary indicators.
  - **Chartmetric platform ranks and Where People Listen geography** — Chartmetric produces these via its own models. We ingest the values as-is and treat them as provider-reported observations, with the provider and endpoint recorded on every row.
  - **Shazam chart positions** — Shazam's own ranking output; ingested, not modelled by NMAS.
- **Where AI *was* used (development-time only, outside the data path):** source discovery for the References file, drafting of methodology documents, and developer productivity tooling. These activities do not touch the extraction, storage, or aggregation pipeline and do not influence any published figure. Details on Slide T11.
- **Why this matters for NBS:** for official statistics, deterministic and auditable beats model-derived. Naming the exact points where third-party ML outputs enter the dataset is part of that auditability.

**Speaker notes:** State plainly: no AI model produced any number in this delivery. The only model outputs in the data are Spotify's popularity index, Chartmetric's platform ranks, and Shazam's chart positions — all third-party, all clearly labelled as provider-reported, and none used in monetary indicator formulas. If a reviewer asks "how much of this is AI-generated," the answer is: zero in the monetary indicators, and the model-derived third-party inputs are disclosed on this slide.
**Visual:** Three-column table — "Deterministic (NMAS pipeline)" / "Third-party model outputs we ingest" / "AI used only in development (non-production)". Use a clear status pill on each row.

## Slide T6 — Indicator Formulas (Auditable)
**Content (mathematical form, mapping each term to a stored variable):**

Let, for an artist `a` and quarter `q`:
- `S_a,q` = mean of daily Spotify monthly listeners (variable `Spotify_monthly_listeners_daily`, rule `net_change` for QoQ Δ; mean here is for the proxy step, see implementation).
- `Y_a,q` = sum of daily YouTube artist views (variable `YouTube_artist_daily_views`, rule `sum`).
- `D_a,q` = mean of daily Deezer fans (variable `Deezer_fans_daily`, rule `net_change`).

Then:
- **Streaming revenue (USD):** `R_a,q = (S_a,q × 3.5 × 3 × $0.004) + (Y_a,q × $0.004) + (D_a,q × 2 × 3 × $0.004) + 0.30 × (S_a,q × 3.5 × 3 × $0.004)`.
- **Streaming revenue (NGN):** `R_a,q × 1500`.
- **Export revenue (USD):** `0.70 × Σ_a R_a,q` (panel-level 70% applied).
- **Employment:** `E_q = E_{q-1} × 1.02`, with `E_{Q1 2025} = 1,300,000`; M/F split 0.62 / 0.38.
- **Costs:** `C_q = 131 × 2 × (₦750,000 + ₦15,000 + ₦50,000 + ₦250,000) = ₦272,480,000`.
